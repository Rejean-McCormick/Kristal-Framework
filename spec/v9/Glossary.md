# Glossary

**Logical Artifact** — domain-native logical content governed by a named contract.

**Logical Commitment** — digest of normalized logical content under a named profile.

**Blob Digest** — digest of exact physical bytes.

**State Snapshot** — immutable composition of logical members and pinned references.

**Derivation** — declared transformation from exact inputs to outputs.

**Materialization** — physical representation of a logical artifact.

**Segment** — immutable physical storage unit.

**Shard** — semantic/ownership/lifecycle boundary.

**Exchange** — portable package of state and materializations.

**Runtime Pack** — rebuildable optimized read/compute representation.
