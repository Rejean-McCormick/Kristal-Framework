# Kristal Standard 7.0 — Core specification

**Status: 7.0.0-draft.3.2.**

## 1. Terminology
Kristal = portable unit. Kristall = encyclopedic meta-orchestrator.
## 2. Compatibility foundation
Valid v6 `kristal_state` artifacts remain valid and unchanged.
## 3. Kristall root
A `kristall_manifest` or equivalent root descriptor identifies namespace, sources, registries and policies.
## 4. Source Kristals
Source identity, assertions, provenance, evidence, applicability and conflicts remain preserved.
## 5. Global semantic identity
Kristall supports opaque KQ/KP/KA/KS identities scoped to a namespace; external IDs remain mappings.
## 6. Assertion families
Repeated assertions may be factorized without deleting source membership/provenance.
## 7. Semantic resonance
Resonance is a vector/candidate signal and cannot establish identity, equivalence, truth, causality or authority.
## 8. Mesh
Typed edges must distinguish factual, structural, identity-resolution, similarity, derivation, conflict and lineage semantics. Mesh paths do not automatically become assertions.
## 9. Subject regions
Kristall may define stable subject regions independent of orientation.

## 10. Knowledge organization and TOC

Kristall MUST distinguish **Category**, **Subject**, **Axis Type** and **Orientation Axis**. A Category is a navigational/classification region; a Subject is a semantic territory; an Axis Type is a reusable organizing grammar; an Orientation Axis is a concrete orientation used for projection.

Kristall SHOULD support one or more external Knowledge Organization Systems through a KOS registry. The default reference-spine profile defined by this draft is the UNESCO Thesaurus. External KOS identifiers/URIs MUST remain external mappings; they MUST NOT replace KQ identity.

Subjects MUST be allowed to be polyhierarchical and MAY have multiple category placements. Classification into a microthesaurus MUST be distinguishable from exact concept equivalence.

The reference TOC MAY treat geographic/country groupings as facets rather than disciplines while preserving the external KOS structure faithfully.

## 11. Orientation axes
An active axis has explicit semantics and affects projection selection/organization. One primary plus at most one secondary axis per portable projection.
## 12. Surfaces
A Surface is a query-time oriented view and may contain gaps/conflicts.
## 13. Portable projections
Materialized Surfaces should remain v6 `kristal_state` with `extensions.kristal_v7`.
## 14. Crystallization
Structural promotions are traceable and auditable.
## 15. Deduplication
Dedup is provenance-preserving factorization; duplicated organization may reveal axes.
## 16. Epistemic separation
Source assertion != derived assertion != structural relation != similarity signal != hypothesis != path.
## 17. Determinism
Rebuildable projections declare source snapshot, subject, axes, policies and transform version.
## 18. Canonicalization
v7 meta-artifacts use a separate versioned canonicalization profile; v6 states retain v6 rules.
## 19. Implementation neutrality
No specific graph/vector/AI technology is required.
## 20. Reconstruction
Implementations should test crystallization by reconstructing known oriented Kristals.
## 21. Recursive factorization
Meta-objects may participate in higher-order structure while preserving lower-level provenance.
