# What is Kristal v7?

Kristal v7 is a **meta-orchestration and knowledge-factorization standard** layered over the v6 portable state model.

## One-sentence definition

> Kristal v7 lets many independently valid Kristals be aligned inside Kristall, factorized into a shared Mesh and orientation axes, and projected back into portable Kristals without erasing provenance, disagreement, scope or source identity.

## Why v7 exists

As a corpus grows, many Kristals begin to overlap. They may share the same subject but organize it under radically different orientations: legal, financial, historical, causal, social, mathematical, operational, cultural, geographic or other domain-defined axes.

Copying shared concepts into every Kristal causes uncontrolled redundancy. Merging all Kristals into one document destroys their useful orientation. v7 instead treats overlap as structure to be discovered.

A dense region can evolve conceptually as:

```text
bridges → Mesh → apparent Surface → cross-surface factorization → Kristall structure
```

The process is not required to be linear and MUST NOT manufacture continuity where evidence or structure is missing.

## The key shift

In v6, the portable state is the primary unit of composition.

In v7, a portable Kristal can also be understood as a **projection** from Kristall. The same subject can therefore generate many valid Kristals under different orientations without creating parallel sources of truth.
