# Workflow Example: Recipes

Recipes illustrate why v6 generalizes the old certainty concept.

A recipe inventory is not primarily a step-by-step cookbook. It can model recipe identity, variants, ingredients, techniques and cultural context.

## Ingredient valuation

For ingredient-membership assertions, an ordinal valuation might mean **constitutive necessity**:

```text
established  defining / indispensable
high         core
medium       typical but substitutable
low          common/peripheral
```

That is not a probability that the ingredient exists.

## Coordinates

Useful coordinates include:

- tradition;
- region;
- variant family;
- preparation structure;
- ingredient function.

## Historical assertions

Origin/history assertions can use a different valuation dimension such as documentary support. The assertion family determines the dimension.

## Substitutions

An ingredient substitution can be acceptable in one variant family and incompatible with a stricter traditional definition. Preserve both the valuation and the conflict/applicability context.
