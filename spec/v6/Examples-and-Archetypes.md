# Examples & Archetypes

Kristal domains vary, but many share recurring reasoning shapes.

| Archetype | Typical valuation dimension | Example domains |
|---|---|---|
| formal | proof / justification closure | mathematics |
| diagnostic | support of competing hypotheses | automotive, software, mineral identification |
| pathway | applicability of transitions/rules | education, business startup |
| identity | constitutive importance | recipes, musical styles |
| maintenance | intervention necessity | buildings, industrial assets |
| taxonomic | acceptance/classification | botany, paleontology |
| attribution | attribution strength | art, archaeology, archives |
| compatibility | constraint satisfaction | engineering, parts, software |
| historical | documentary robustness | history of technology |
| multiscale | relation across layers/scales | human body |

## Examples

### Recipes

`valuations[]` can encode constitutive necessity of an ingredient while evidence separately explains why that interpretation is supported.

### Automotive diagnosis

Symptoms, tests and observations support or contradict causal hypotheses. A DTC is evidence; it is not automatically the failed part.

### Quebec business pathway

Official obligations are authoritative constraints; project facts are observed state; local business policies are organizational rules. Applicability derives the relevant institutional path.

### Botany

Taxonomic acceptance and geographic occurrence confidence are different valuation dimensions. Competing taxonomic circumscriptions can coexist rather than being flattened.

### Home maintenance

Season/climate, building component, observed condition and intervention necessity are separable. Manufacturer guidance and legal requirements remain distinct authority channels.
