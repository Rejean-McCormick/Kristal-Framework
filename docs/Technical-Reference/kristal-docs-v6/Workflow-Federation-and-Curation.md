# Workflow: Federation & Curation

Federation combines multiple sources or Kristal States without erasing disagreement.

## Preserve source identity

Each assertion should remain traceable to the source state/evidence/provenance that produced it.

## Preserve semantic dimensions

Do not merge assertions merely because their text looks similar when they differ in:

- valuation dimension;
- coordinates;
- applicability;
- record role;
- validation/recognition;
- conflict or succession relationship.

## Competing assertions

Conflicting taxonomies, historical interpretations, diagnostics or organizational policies may coexist. Use explicit incompatibility/conflict edges and applicability rather than choosing a silent winner.

## Supersession

When a rule or classification is replaced, preserve the older assertion with its time/applicability and link the newer one via succession/supersession rather than deleting history.
