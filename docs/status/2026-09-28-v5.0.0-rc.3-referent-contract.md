## Scope of rc.3

rc.3 adds a deliberately small, domain-neutral referent identity layer. It does not replace or reinterpret the Structured Epistemic State. Assertions, evidence, provenance, certainty, validation and authority remain in the existing epistemic-state contracts.

The Referent Registry fixes only a shallow set of interoperable kinds and stable internal `ref` values. `person` and `collective` are the actor-oriented distinctions in the profile; domain-specific subclasses remain classifications/extensions rather than new core ontology branches.

Concepts are valid referents but are not promoted to a mandatory navigation axis. A consumer may choose people-first, process-first, place-first, work-first or another domain-appropriate view without changing Kristal semantics.

External identifiers such as Wikidata, VIAF or Project Gutenberg remain external anchors and do not replace internal refs or confer epistemic authority.

## Documentary modeling

The profile explicitly preserves the distinction:

```text
work != edition != manifestation
```

This supports intellectual/cultural corpora without forcing source-provider/file identities to stand in for the work itself.

## Compatibility

- Existing v5 Structured Epistemic State documents remain valid.
- No existing enum or assertion field is removed or reinterpreted.
- Consumers do not need Referent Registry support unless they claim the new profile.
- rc.2 status documents remain historical records.
