# Kristal Standard 7.0.0-draft.3.1 — UNESCO TOC alignment

Draft.3 adds the first explicit encyclopedic TOC.

- UNESCO Thesaurus becomes the default external reference-spine KOS.
- The local profile mirrors 7 domains and 88 microthesauri.
- Category, Subject, Axis Type and Orientation Axis are now distinct normative concepts.
- Subjects are polyhierarchical.
- UNESCO URIs remain external; KQ remains Kristall identity.
- Domain 7 (countries/groupings) defaults to a facet family in Kristall navigation.
- 18 native Axis Types are seeded from the structural patterns exposed by the 100-Kristal corpus.
- 100 Subject KQs are seeded with UNESCO microthesaurus placements and preferred Axis Types.
- v6 `kristal_state` remains unchanged and backward compatible.
