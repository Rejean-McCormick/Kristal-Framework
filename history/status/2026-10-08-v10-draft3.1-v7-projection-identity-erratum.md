# Kristal v10.0.0-draft.3.1 — v7 portable projection identity erratum

Draft.3.1 is a maintenance release. It does not change the v6 portable epistemic contract, v7 semantic model, v8 query/language layer, v9 semantic-state architecture, or the v10 hosted-network/read-surface contracts.

The inherited `v6-compatible-projection.example.json` fixture carried the base v6 example identity even though the projection adds `extensions.kristal_v7`. Under the unchanged v6 `kristal.v6:jcs-rfc8785` rule, extensions remain in the hash target. The correct identity is therefore:

```text
sha256:9f3fdad6b298e47a3f8750f418899f9208c4e3691f20233648abde2e2188bd79
```

The historical v8/v9 compatibility-lock documents are not rewritten. The exact correction is declared in `contracts/compatibility-errata.json`, and conformance now verifies that a documented erratum matches both the historical locked bytes and the corrected active fixture. The reference tests also verify the portable projection identity directly so this defect cannot recur silently.
