# Kristal Standard v7 — draft.2 semantic identity and resonance

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Version:** 7.0.0-draft.2

Draft.2 retains every draft.1 architectural layer and adds:

1. opaque Kristall semantic IDs: `KQ`, `KP`, `KA`, `KS`;
2. explicit mapping of Wikidata and Orgo semantic charter identifiers as external IDs;
3. a shared Semantic Resonance Contract usable by Orgo and Kristall;
4. resonance as a vector/candidate signal, never authority or truth;
5. property, assertion and source registries;
6. KQ-backed orientation axes and KP-backed Mesh relations;
7. expanded v7 validation vectors.

The v6 `kristal_state` schema remains unchanged. The v7 portable projection test continues to validate against that unchanged schema.
